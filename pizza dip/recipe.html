Pizza Dip

Easy and delicious Pizza Dip recipe

Ingredients

For the Pull-Apart Bread

  • 16.3 ounce can Grands biscuit dough
  • 1/4 cup melted butter
  • 2 teaspoons parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning

For the Pizza Dip

  • 8 ounces cream cheese
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 3 teaspoons Italian seasoning
  • 1 cup marinara sauce
  • 1 1/2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 8 slices pepperoni

Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.

2. Cut each biscuit in half, then roll each half into a ball.

3. Mix melted butter, parmesan cheese and Italian seasoning.

4. Roll the biscuit balls in the butter mixture and arrange them around the edge of a skillet.

5. Mix the cream cheese, ricotta cheese and Italian seasoning.

6. Spread the cream cheese mixture in the middle of the skillet.

7. Add marinara sauce, mozzarella cheese and pepperoni.

8. Bake for 25-30 minutes.
